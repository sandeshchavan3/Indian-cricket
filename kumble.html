Anil Kumble

Personal Information

Full Name: Anil Kumble

Born: October 17, 1970

Birthplace: Bangalore, Karnataka, India

Bowling Style: Right-arm leg spin

Role: Bowler

Key Records

  • 619 Test wickets – 2nd highest for India
  • Only Indian to take 10 wickets in a Test innings (10/74)
  • Once the fastest to 250 Test wickets
  • Notable for his precision and consistency
  • Numerous five-wicket hauls in Test cricket
  • Key in drawing out long bowling spells
  • Awarded the Padma Shri and Padma Bhushan
  • Paved the way for future spin bowlers
  • Enjoyed a long and illustrious international career
  • Later served as a mentor and coach for younger players
