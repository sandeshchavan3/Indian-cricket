Sachin Tendulkar

Personal Information

Full Name: Sachin Ramesh Tendulkar

Born: April 24, 1973

Birthplace: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India

Batting Style: Right-hand bat

Role: Batsman

Key Records

  • 100 international centuries
  • Most runs in international cricket
  • Most centuries in Tests
  • First double century in ODI
  • Highest number of half-centuries
  • Most international matches played
  • Fastest century for India in ODIs
  • Record-breaking career spanning decades
  • Inspiration for generations of cricketers
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor
