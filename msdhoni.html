MS Dhoni

Full Name: Mahendra Singh Dhoni

Date of Birth: July 7, 1981

Role: Wicketkeeper Batsman

Batting Style: Right-hand bat

Bowling Style: Right-arm medium

Nationality: Indian

Team(s): India, Chennai Super Kings

ODI Debut: December 23, 2004 vs Bangladesh

Test Debut: December 2, 2005 vs Sri Lanka

T20I Debut: December 1, 2006 vs South Africa

Records:

  • Led India to 2007 T20 World Cup, 2011 ODI World Cup, 2013 Champions Trophy victories
  • Most international matches as captain for India
  • First Indian wicketkeeper to score 4000+ Test runs
  • Only captain to win all three ICC trophies
  • Fastest stumping record (0.08 sec)
  • Most dismissals in T20Is by an Indian wicketkeeper
  • Over 10,000 ODI runs
  • Captain of Chennai Super Kings with multiple IPL titles
  • Padma Bhushan, Padma Shri and Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna recipient
